A 3D-printable UFO lamp that brings a playful alien-abduction scene to your desk. It features a domed cockpit, eight separate thrusters, and a green tractor-beam shade, with either a cow silhouette cutout or a plain finish. Magnetic saucer halves and a lift-off cockpit make the interior easy to access, while separate parts let you mix colors. Designed for a Ø60 × 8 mm LED light module, supplied separately, it casts light down through the shade for a sci-fi accent on a desk, shelf, or bedside table.
